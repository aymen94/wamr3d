Water

A 128 × 128 height field with waves, rain and floating balls. Move the pointer over the pool to make ripples, drag to orbit.

How it differs from the original
  • The original computes the height field on the GPU with GPUComputationRenderer and draws it with a custom ShaderMaterial; WAMR3D has neither. Here the wave equation runs in JavaScript and updates the positions and normals of a plain BufferGeometry every frame.
  • The water is a MeshStandardMaterial without reflections, since environment maps are not wired into the object API yet. Balls stand in for the floating objects.
