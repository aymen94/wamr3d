City 3D

A procedural night city with shadowed towers, 60 light streaks on GSAP tweens, floating particles, fog and a grid. Move the mouse to tilt it.

Original: City 3D by Victor Vergara (CodePen) (MIT). Ported to WAMR3D by changing the library.

What changed besides the import
  • Written for three.js r89: CubeGeometry → BoxGeometry, the removed shading option dropped. GSAP TweenMax 1.20.3 is loaded as in the original.
  • MeshToonMaterial → MeshLambertMaterial, MeshPhongMaterial → MeshStandardMaterial; the 3 % wireframe overlay is left out (no wireframes yet).
  • PCF shadows with a 2048 map instead of PCFSoft at 6000.
