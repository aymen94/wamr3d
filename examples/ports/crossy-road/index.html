Crossy Road

Hop across endless roads and forests with the arrow keys (or the buttons). Cars, trucks, shadows and canvas textures.

Original: Crossy Road tutorial by Hunor Márton Borbély (tutorial code). Ported to WAMR3D by changing the library.

What changed besides the import
  • Box3.setFromObject is not in WAMR3D yet: the hit test builds the boxes from world positions and known sizes.
  • WebGLRenderer → WebGPURenderer, and an opaque background instead of a transparent canvas.
