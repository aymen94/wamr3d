160,000 triangles

One BufferGeometry with 160,000 separate triangles, flat normals and vertex colours, lit by two lights through fog.

Original: three.js example webgl_buffergeometry (MIT, © three.js authors). Ported to WAMR3D by changing the library.

What changed besides the import
  • MeshPhongMaterial → MeshStandardMaterial with low roughness.
  • Vertex colours have three components: the random per-triangle alpha is dropped.
