Car

A car on a moving grid with spinning wheels. Pick the body, details and glass colours below, drag to orbit.

Inspired by the three.js example webgl_materials_car. This is a reduced version written for WAMR3D, not a port of its code.

How it differs from the original
  • The original loads a Ferrari 458 model (glTF with Draco compression). WAMR3D has no glTF loader yet, so this car is built from boxes and cylinders in code.
  • MeshPhysicalMaterial (clear coat, glass transmission) and the HDR environment are not available. The paint is a MeshStandardMaterial under ordinary lights and the glass is transparent.
  • A blurred canvas texture stands in for the baked shadow texture.
