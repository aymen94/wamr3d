Spotlights

Three coloured spot lights with PCF shadows, tweened to new angles, penumbras and positions every five seconds. Drag to orbit.

Original: three.js example webgl_lights_spotlights (MIT, © three.js authors). Ported to WAMR3D by changing the library.

What changed besides the import
  • MeshPhongMaterial → MeshLambertMaterial; no SpotLightHelper cones.
  • OrbitControls is a small stand-in in controls.js; tween.js comes from a CDN instead of three/addons.
