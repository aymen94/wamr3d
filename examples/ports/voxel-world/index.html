Voxel world

A 128 × 128 Minecraft-style terrain from Perlin noise, merged into one mesh. Move the mouse to look; W/S, arrows or mouse buttons to fly.

Original: three.js example webgl_geometry_minecraft (MIT, © three.js authors). Ported to WAMR3D by changing the library.

What changed besides the import
  • Faces are merged in the page instead of cloning one geometry per block with the BufferGeometryUtils add-on (WAMR3D geometries have no clone() yet).
  • The texture atlas is painted on a canvas with the same layout; ImprovedNoise is the three.js add-on, copied.
  • FirstPersonControls is a small stand-in in controls.js.
