Fluid particles

About 4,000 particles of liquid in a tilting tank, coloured by speed. Move the pointer sideways to tilt the tank, double-click to splash, drag to orbit.

Inspired by the three.js example webgpu_compute_particles_fluid. This is a reduced version written for WAMR3D, not a port of its code.

How it differs from the original
  • The original simulates on the GPU with TSL compute shaders, which WAMR3D does not have. Here the simulation runs in JavaScript on the CPU (fluid.js), with fewer particles.
  • The particles are drawn as one InstancedMesh of small spheres, with instance colours from their speed.
